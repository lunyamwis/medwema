Everything you need

A Complete Clinic in Your Browser

From the moment a patient walks in to the final receipt — Medwema handles every step, with no paper required.

Patient Management

Register patients, manage visit history, track demographics, and handle patient queues with real-time updates.

Electronic Medical Records

Full consultation records, diagnoses, doctor notes, and patient history accessible in seconds.

Billing & Payments

Generate itemised bills, accept Paystack payments, track outstanding balances, and issue receipts instantly.

Lab & Diagnostics

Order lab tests, manage lab queues, record results with reference ranges, and link findings to consultations.

Prescription Management

Prescribe medications linked to inventory, track dispensing status, and control drug quantities automatically.

Pharmacy Inventory

Track drug stock levels, manage purchase orders, set reorder alerts, and monitor consumption patterns.

Specialist Services

Sonography studies, nursing notes, home visits, debt management, and external lab requests — all tracked.

Analytics & Reports

Live dashboards with Chart.js charts — revenue trends, patient flow, lab volumes, and inventory metrics.

Real-time Notifications

WebSocket-powered alerts and browser push notifications keep your entire team informed instantly.

REST API

Full OpenAPI-documented REST API for integrations — connect lab equipment, mobile apps, or third-party tools.

Multi-Clinic Support

Manage multiple branches under one account. Data is fully isolated per clinic — no cross-clinic leakage.

Security & Audit

Role-based access control, full change history via django-simple-history, and CSRF-protected endpoints.
